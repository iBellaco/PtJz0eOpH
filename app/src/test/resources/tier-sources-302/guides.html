How we rank champions

We rank champions based on the current Wild Rift ranked meta, with separate tiers for each role. Our commentary explains the patch changes, item synergies and matchups behind the champions we recommend right now.

Patch 7.3B: Biggest risers & hot picks

Reviewed by Royal · 15 Oct 2026
  • Samira · Dragon Lane

    Samira remains an S+ ADC in our Wild Rift Patch 7.3B tier list. Her Patch 7.3A buffs improved her damage and durability, rewarding aggressive Dragon lane players who follow allied engages and turn close-range teamfights in their favor.

  • Sona · Support

    Sona is one of the best Wild Rift supports for Patch 7.3B as repeated Senna nerfs open space for scaling enchanters. Echoes of Helia, Diadem of Songs and Harmonic Echo strengthen her late-game healing and shielding, making her especially hard to outlast in grouped teamfights.

  • Hwei · Mid Lane

    Hwei remains an S-tier mid laner in Wild Rift Patch 7.3B despite further nerfs to his damage, shielding and crowd control. His waveclear, ranged pressure and area control still make him a strong pick for controlling objective fights.

  • Malphite · Baron Lane

    Malphite remains an S+ Baron lane pick in our Wild Rift Patch 7.3B tier list. His armor scaling and powerful engage make him a strong answer to attack-damage carries, combining frontline durability with an ultimate that can decide a teamfight.

  • Skarner · Jungle

    Skarner is a standout tank jungler in Wild Rift Patch 7.3B. The jungle changes make his previously weak clear speed more competitive, helping him keep pace in gold while bringing reliable ganks and a durable frontline. A well-timed Impale can catch multiple carries and decide a teamfight.

Wild Rift Meta: Patch 7.3

Patch 7.3 reshaped the Wild Rift meta through marksman buffs, new items, and jungle and turret changes. These are the main trends behind our Wild Rift tier list and the best champions to consider for each role.

Dragon lane / ADC meta

Stronger on-hit items, attack speed buffs and increased critical strike damage have made ADCs the centrepiece of the meta. Both on-hit and crit marksmen offer strong picks in the Dragon lane tier list, with sustained damage shaping teamfights.

Mid lane meta

Mages lead the mid lane meta, including the newest champion, Hwei. Their prominence also rewards anti-mage picks such as Kassadin, making the matchup especially important when choosing a mid laner.

Jungle meta

The Smite and jungle changes have strengthened utility and tank junglers. Reliable engage, crowd control and frontline presence are valuable, while many crit junglers that rely on heavy farming are struggling to match their impact.

Baron lane / top lane meta

Turret changes reward strong 1v1 champions and skirmishers, while tanks also excel in Baron lane. Malphite stands out with a high pick rate and win rate, making him a defining pick in the current top lane meta.

Support meta

New support items have strengthened both engage supports and enchanters. The support tier list offers strong options for starting fights or protecting your ADC; choose the style that complements your team's damage and frontline.
